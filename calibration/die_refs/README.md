# Die-photo physical references

Measured block geometry for kiln's reference designs, from public die photos, published floorplans and package photos.
One JSON per chip (`<chip>.json`), an overlay of the measured boxes (`overlay_<chip>.png`), and the tools that produced them.
Everything here is regenerated by `python3 calibration/die_refs/tools/build.py` from the pixel readings in `tools/specs.py`.

## Method

1. **Scale.** Each die image is scaled so its area equals the published area (A100 826, P100 610, H100 814 mm2) at the aspect
   ratio measured on the photo. Independent checks: Kurnal's caliper for GA100 is 25.65 x 32.35 mm (we use 25.58 x 32.30);
   Fritz's caliper for GP100 is 22.499 x 27.471 mm = 618 mm2 (1.3% above published; we keep his aspect, scale to 610).
2. **Rectification.** GP100's only full-die photo is angled; `tools/rectify.py` maps its four top-surface corners onto the
   die rectangle (homography). Residual error is ~1% of size.
3. **Reading blocks.** Boxes were read by eye on downscaled frames and refined with brightness/colour profiles (PHY strips,
   SRAM-dense bands). Coordinates in `tools/specs.py` are image pixels; `build.py` converts to mm with origin bottom-left.
   Every block has a `confidence` and a `source` saying whether it comes from an annotation/paper or from my reading of
   repeated structure. Boxes tile the die with small overlaps/gaps; `coverage_frac` in each JSON gives the sum.
4. **Package.** Stack counts and placement from package photos/renders; sizes scaled from the die (or from the HBM2 KGSD
   footprint, 7.75 x 11.87 mm, as a cross-check).
5. **Validation.** On TPU v4i the redrawn Fig. 6 gives CMEM 27.0% (paper: 28%) and MXUs 11.1% (paper: 11%).

## Physical organisation (what the photos agree on)

* **HBM on the two long edges, I/O on the short edges** for GA100 (3+3 HBM; SerDes 3 + 1), GP100 (2+2; NVLink and PCIe both on
  one short edge) and, from renders/package photos, GH100 (3+3) and GV100 (2+2). TPU v4 package: 2+2 on the long edges.
  TPU v4i is different: both HBM controllers/PHYs on one edge, ICI and PCIe on the opposite edge.
* **L2 sits in bands between SM rows, with a crossbar macro at the centre of each band.** GA100: two full-width bands at ~25%
  and ~70% of die height, each between two GPC rows (2 GPC columns x 4 rows); ~20 slice-like units per half band, ~80 total,
  matching 80 x 512 KB slices. GP100: one central band between two GPC rows (3 GPC columns x 2 rows). The GH100 and GV100 renders
  show the same pattern (two bands with a central hub). NVIDIA's block diagrams draw one L2 band; that is logical, not physical.
* **Package:** A100: 6 HBM2 sites, 3 per side, stack long axis parallel to the die long edge, interposer ~44.7 x 37.8 mm,
  substrate ~54 x 54 mm (from NVIDIA's render, low). H100: 6 sites, 3 per side; die aspect 0.827. P100: 4 stacks 7.527 x
  11.490 mm, 2 per side, package 55 x 55 mm (Fritz). TPU v4: die ~23.1 x 25.7 mm (593 mm2, +-4%) with 2+2 HBM2.
  A100 40GB / H100 SXM5 80GB use 5 of 6 sites; **which site is unused cannot be seen in any public photo** (all six look alike).

## Where kiln's reference designs disagree (designs read-only; nothing changed)

* **L2 placement (A100, H100, V100, P100).** kiln declares L2 partitions as a separate grid (the A100 one ends up below all
  SMs, per the brief). Real dies: L2 bands *between* GPC rows with a central crossbar; GA100 has two such bands (one per
  partition), GP100 one.
* **NVLink/PCIe edges.** kiln: NVLink on S, PCIe on N for A100/H100/V100/P100. GA100: three NVLink-like SerDes macros on one short
  edge, the 4th (likely PCIe) on the opposite short edge. GP100: NVLink *and* the probable PCIe macro on the same short edge.
  GV100 render: I/O on one short edge. HBM on W/E (3+3, 2+2) agrees everywhere.
* **Outlines.** A100 25.6 x 32.3 agrees. P100 kiln 22.1 x 27.6 (0.80) vs photo 0.819 (22.35 x 27.29). H100 kiln 25.3 x 32.2
  (0.786) vs package photo 0.827 (25.9 x 31.4). TPU v4 kiln 24 x 24 square vs ~23.1 x 25.7 (0.90).
* **Uncore.** kiln assumes 60 mm2 of misc. Clearly non-SM/non-L2/non-PHY logic on GA100 is ~22 mm2 on the south edge plus
  ~40 mm2 between HBM PHYs (memory-controller candidates) and ~10 mm2 of north I/O misc; GP100 ~38 mm2 + 27 mm2.
* **Harvested HBM site.** kiln disables `hbm5`; the physical site is not identifiable from public images (no disagreement
  provable, but no support either).
* **TPU v4i.** kiln has no shoreline; the paper's floorplan has HBM on one edge only, ICI + PCIe on the opposite edge, CMEM split
  into two halves above/below a central TensorCore row (MXU | MXU | XLU | VPU/VMEM | XLU | MXU | MXU).

## Files

* `<chip>.json`: `{chip, die, blocks[{id, kind, count, bbox_mm, area_mm2, area_each_mm2, frac, confidence, source}], edges,
  package, transistors, node, images, notes, kiln_disagreements, coverage_frac}`. bbox is `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` in mm, origin
  bottom-left; a list of those when `count > 1`.
* `overlay_<chip>.png`: boxes on the photo (GA100, GP100, TPU v4) or a schematic redraw (GH100, TPU v4i) when the source is
  copyrighted. `overlay_<chip>_photo.png` (gitignored) is the overlay on the copyrighted source, regenerated locally if
  `img/nonfree/` is present. GV100 has no overlay (nothing measured).
* `img/`: downscaled copies of the free images. Full-resolution originals and `img/nonfree/` are gitignored; their URLs are in
  each JSON's `images` and below.
* `tools/rectify.py` (homography rectification), `tools/grid.py` (mm grid for reading), `tools/specs.py` (readings),
  `tools/build.py` (JSON + overlays).
